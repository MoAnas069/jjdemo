Effective Date: 3/7/2025

1. SMS Program Overview

By opting in to text messaging from Texas Gold Realty, you agree to receive recurring text messages regarding real estate updates, property inquiry responses, appointment scheduling, and customer support. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any property or service.

2. Opt-In & Consent

You may opt into receiving text messages by submitting your mobile number and checking the SMS consent box on our contact or inquiry forms. By opting in, you represent that you are the account holder or authorized user of the phone number provided.

3. Opt-Out & Unsubscribe Instructions

You can cancel the SMS service at any time. To opt out and stop receiving SMS messages from Texas Gold Realty:

  • Text STOP to any text message you receive from us.
  • Upon sending STOP, you will receive a single confirmation message stating that you have been unsubscribed. No further messages will be sent unless you re-subscribe.
  • To resume receiving text messages after opting out, reply START or submit a new inquiry form on our website.

4. Help & Support

If you experience any issues or have questions regarding our SMS messaging program:

  • Text HELP to any text message from us to receive assistance information.
  • Call our registered support phone number directly at (713) 825-5281.
  • Email customer support at joel@realtorTX.org.

5. Message Frequency & Rates

Message frequency may vary based on your interaction with Texas Gold Realty, property updates, and service requests. Standard message and data rates may apply for any text messages sent to or received from us according to your mobile plan with your wireless carrier.

7. Privacy & Data Protection

We respect your privacy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. For more information, please view our Privacy Policy.
